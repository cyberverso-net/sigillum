### Changed

- Threat K6 of the threat model assesses the cryptographic policy against ETSI TS 119 312 V2.1.1 (2026-06), Cryptographic Suites, and states where sigillum differs from it: creation with RSA keys of 2048 to 2999 bits has no end date, DSA is rejected, the other listed mechanisms (Ed448, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, LMS, XMSS, the Brainpool and FRP256v1 curves, EC-SDSA-opt and SHA-224) are not implemented, and SHA-1 is accepted as a lookup identifier. A dated note in ADR-0023 records that the same specification lists Ed25519 as recommended with no end date, which supports the Ed25519 delta of the aligned policy of `tools/dss-oracle`.
