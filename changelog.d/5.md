### Added

- Dependency audits with `cargo vet`, checked in continuous integration. The audits of Mozilla, Google and the Bytecode Alliance are imported. Tooling crates need only `safe-to-run`.
- RustCrypto and dalek crates are trusted until 2027-10-24 when released by the publishers that crates.io recorded for their releases from 2022-01-01 to 2026-10-24. The set is the crates that `cargo vet` sees for the planned RustCrypto and dalek dependencies, with default features and the `cms` builder.
- For the tooling crates, publishers that Mozilla or the Bytecode Alliance trust are trusted for `safe-to-run`. The remaining dependencies are exempted until the review before release 0.1 (#63).
