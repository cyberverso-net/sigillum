### Added

- `sigillum-testpki generate pki`, which writes the test PKI under `testdata/pki/` and records each certificate and private key in the manifest. It removes the entries of certificates it no longer produces and reports files under `testdata/pki/` that the manifest does not name. The test PKI has a root CA, one issuing CA per signature family (RSA PKCS#1 v1.5, RSASSA-PSS, ECDSA, Ed25519) and a second ECDSA CA with a P-521 key, end-entity signing certificates for every signature family in scope, including ECDSA on P-521 with SHA-512, and for SHA3-256, a timestamping authority, an OCSP responder, and expired, not yet valid and misconfigured cases: an end entity without `nonRepudiation`, CA certificates with `cA` FALSE and without basic constraints, and an end entity with an unknown critical extension. Generation is reproducible byte for byte.
- The committed test PKI under `testdata/pki/`, with its private keys under `testdata/pki/keys/` as PKCS#8 files named `*.test-key.pem`. They are test keys with no value.
- The sigillum test arc of object identifiers, `1.3.6.1.4.1.32473.1`, under the documentation Enterprise Number of RFC 5612, with its allocation table in `testdata/README.md`.
- `clippy.toml`, which forbids RSA private-key types and functions in library crates, and the matching `disallowed_types` and `disallowed_methods` workspace lints, as ADR-0010 requires.

### Security

- `deny.toml` accepts RUSTSEC-2023-0071 for the `rsa` crate and RUSTSEC-2026-0387 for `crypto-bigint` 0.5, under ADR-0020.
