# Governance

## Current model

sigillum is a personal open-source project hosted in the `cyberverso-net` GitHub organisation. It is led by a single maintainer, Paolo De Rosa, who has final responsibility for technical decisions, releases and the contents of the documents in `docs/`.

Decisions are made in the open: in issues and pull requests for day-to-day matters, and through a change to the relevant document for anything affecting the [charter](docs/project/charter.md), [scope](docs/project/scope.md) or [roadmap](docs/project/roadmap.md). Such changes are proposed in a pull request and left open for comment for at least 7 days before being merged.

## Maintainers

| Name | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Paolo De Rosa | Lead maintainer |

Contributors who make sustained, high-quality contributions may be invited to become maintainers. Maintainers may approve and merge pull requests; changes to the charter, scope, licence or governance require the lead maintainer's approval.

## Independence

The project is developed in a personal capacity and is independent of any employer, public authority or trust service provider. Contributors participating on behalf of an organisation should say so in their pull requests.

## Evolution

If the project gains users and contributors beyond its maintainer, its governance is expected to evolve towards a broader maintainer group and, possibly, a home in a neutral open-source foundation. Any such change will be proposed publicly under the process above.

## Licence and trademarks

The licence (Apache-2.0 OR MIT) is not expected to change. Because there is no contributor licence agreement, a change would require the consent of all copyright holders. The project name is used only to identify this project.
