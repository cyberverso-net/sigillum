### Changed

- The minimum supported Rust version is raised from 1.88 to 1.89, the version required by `aes` 0.9.3 and later. `sigillum-pki` now depends on `aes` 0.9.4 instead of a pin to 0.9.2. The development image installs Rust 1.89 as its minimum version.
- The steps for raising the minimum supported Rust version include the version of the development image.
