### Added

- `sigillum-testpki generate ocsp`, which writes the OCSP responses of the test PKI under `testdata/ocsp/` and records each in the manifest, after `generate pki`. Each certificate with a response links it in `revocation`, after its CRL. The command keeps the `revocation` links of the response entries it replaces, removes the entries of responses it no longer produces, with the links to them, and reports files under `testdata/ocsp/` that the manifest does not name. Generation is reproducible byte for byte.
- The committed OCSP responses under `testdata/ocsp/`, assembled by the `x509-ocsp` response builder with an explicit `producedAt`, as ADR-0014 decides. Every end entity has a `good` or `revoked` response consistent with the CRL of its CA: signed by the delegated responder `pki/ocsp-responder` for the ECDSA CA, and by the issuing CA itself for the others. Further responses have the status `unknown`, a signer without `id-kp-OCSPSigning`, a `producedAt` after the validation time, a nonce, the response statuses `tryLater` and `unauthorized`, and a `CertID` with a wrong issuer name hash or issuer key hash, for threat K10 of the threat model.
