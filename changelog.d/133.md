### Added

- ADR-0027 changes the order of delivery of JAdES. JAdES B-B is developed with CAdES B-B, as the second user of the shared signing API, and JAdES B-T and B-LT follow CAdES B-LT, in parallel with the PDF and PAdES work, instead of after PAdES. Release 0.1 still requires CAdES, PAdES and CMS encryption; JAdES enters it if it meets the conformance, fuzzing and platform criteria of phase 1 before release 0.1 is ready, and never delays it.

### Changed

- The scope, the roadmap and the architecture overview follow ADR-0027. ADR-0008 and ADR-0026 carry dated notes, and the status line of ADR-0008 and its index row name the order of delivery as superseded.
