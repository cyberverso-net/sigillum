### Changed

- The test certificates `pki/signer-rsa-sha3-256`, `pki/signer-rsa-revoked` and `pki/signer-rsa-pss-revoked` have 3072-bit RSA keys instead of 2048-bit ones. ETSI TS 119 312 V2.1.1, Table 6, rates 2048-bit keys as legacy with an end date before the validation time of the corpus. Their keys and certificates are regenerated, and their expected results are unchanged. `pki/signer-rsa-2048` keeps its 2048-bit key as the legacy-key fixture, and its manifest entry says so.
