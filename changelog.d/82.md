### Added

- Dependabot watches the Maven dependencies of `tools/dss-oracle` weekly, in two groups: `dss` for the DSS artefacts, whose updates are oracle changes reviewed against the corpus predictions, and `maven-routine` for the plugins and the other libraries. The Dependabot configuration check now also requires every directory with a `pom.xml` to be listed, and DSS to be kept out of the routine group.
