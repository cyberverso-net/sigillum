### Added

- ADR-0025 builds the phase 1 library code on the `der` 0.8 generation of the RustCrypto crates, with `cms` and `rsa` pinned to their current release candidates and `cms` used without its `builder` feature. sigillum defines the OCSP structures of RFC 6960 and the timestamp structures of RFC 3161 itself. `sigillum-testpki` stays on the `der` 0.7 generation and keeps its own DER length walk, which a differential test compares with the library walk on identifier octets, length octets, nesting and trailing data.
- ADR-0026 sets the conventions of the public signing API: one shape for every format crate (`prepare`, `assemble`, `resume`), states that are never authoritative, with everything signed recomputed at each call from the document, signer chain, options and signing time supplied again and bound by verifying the signature value over it, the unsigned inputs checked against a digest kept in the state, which can only refuse, evidence requests returned to the caller instead of retrieved by the core, explicit signing and validation times, one shared context and error type, and signature values with a stated encoding. For JAdES, digests may replace the objects only with the `ObjectIdByURIHash` mechanism: one digest of the base64url encoding of each object, all with one algorithm. `prepare` never retrieves anything. A signature timestamp is accepted only if its `genTime`, widened by its stated accuracy, does not precede the signing time, which extends rule 6 of ADR-0017. `Signer` and `Fetcher` are public traits for Rust callers, and a generic `sign_with` drives every format crate through a common signing-flow trait.

### Changed

- ADR-0005, ADR-0006, ADR-0011, ADR-0012, ADR-0017, ADR-0020 and ADR-0024 carry dated notes on the parts that ADR-0025 and ADR-0026 change. The status lines of ADR-0005, ADR-0006, ADR-0011, ADR-0012, ADR-0017, ADR-0020 and ADR-0024, and their index rows, name the part that is superseded. The ADR process describes such dated notes and partial supersessions. The acceptance of RUSTSEC-2026-0387 ends for the library crates when they move to the `der` 0.8 generation, and stays for `sigillum-testpki` only.
- The architecture overview, the threat model, the review checklist, the development process and the crate documentation of `sigillum-pki`, `sigillum-cades` and `sigillum-jades` follow ADR-0025 and ADR-0026. In the threat model, boundary B4 and threat G1 cover the assembly state as well as the prepared state. The label table lists `area: jades`, `area: testpki` and `area: measurements`.
