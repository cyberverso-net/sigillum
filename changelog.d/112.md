### Added

- `sigillum-testpki generate documents` writes three PDF documents prepared for the shadow attacks of threat D2, one for each class: hide, replace and hide-and-replace. `generate signed` signs each with DSS at B-B by `pki/signer-p256`, as `signed/pades-shadow-<class>-b-b-p256`.
- `sigillum-testpki generate mutations` derives five PAdES variants that carry an `attack-class`: content added to the page in an incremental update after signing (`incremental-saving`), one shadow attack of each class (`shadow-hide`, `shadow-replace`, `shadow-hide-and-replace`), and a `ByteRange` that skips the signature dictionary in use and covers a copy of the signed bytes (`signature-wrapping`). No signature value is computed again, and the variants are reproduced byte for byte from the committed corpus.
- The divergence DIV-7 in `docs/design/divergences.md`: DSS 6.5.1 passes a PAdES signature whose page content changed in an incremental update after signing, which sigillum is to report as a failure through the revision analysis of phase 2.
- The fuzz target `mutations` also covers the readers of the PAdES variants.
