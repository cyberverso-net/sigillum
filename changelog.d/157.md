### Changed

- The cryptographic policy of the scope follows ETSI TS 119 312 V2.1.1. RSA keys of 3000 bits and above are recommended; RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 and RSA keys of 2048 to 2999 bits are accepted for creation as legacy, and such keys are refused when the signing time is after 2028-12-31. DSA verification is deferred and reported as not supported, and DSA creation stays excluded. Ed448, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, LMS, XMSS, the Brainpool and FRP256v1 curves and SHA-224 on verification are deferred. Expiry dates will come from TS 119 312 V2.1.1, whose normative tables prevail over its Annex D.
