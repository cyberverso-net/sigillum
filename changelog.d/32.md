### Added

- `sigillum-testpki generate mutations` derives malformed variants from the signed corpus and records them under `testdata/mutations/`: a CAdES signature whose signing time changed after signing, a CAdES signature whose encapsulated content changed after signing while its signed attributes stayed intact, a JAdES signature whose protected header changed after signing, and a JAdES signature whose protected header gives `iat` twice. No signature value is computed again. The variants are reproduced byte for byte from the committed corpus, and the workflow `signed-corpus.yml` derives them again after the signed corpus.
- The divergence DIV-6 in `docs/design/divergences.md`: DSS 6.5.1 reports HASH_FAILURE for a JAdES signature whose protected header changed, where ETSI EN 319 102-1 V1.4.1 gives SIG_CRYPTO_FAILURE.
- A fuzz target, `mutations`, for the readers of `generate mutations`.

### Changed

- The format of `testdata/manifest.toml` is now version 3. The optional field `attack-class` names, for a malformed PAdES document, the class of attack on PDF signatures that it carries, as listed in the threat model. It is allowed only on a signed document whose path ends with `.pdf`. The tests of `tools/dss-oracle` read version 3.
