### Added

- Threat model; ADR-0017 on defensive rules for the signing and verification API; ADR-0018 excluding unencoded JAdES payloads and restricting `ObjectIdByURI` to a single object; ADR-0019 defining the classes of divergence from DSS, including intentional restrictions, and restricting Ed25519 public keys of small order.

### Changed

- Unencoded JAdES payloads, the JAdES `HttpHeaders` mechanism and encrypted PDF documents excluded from the scope.
- Scope: CMS encryption is to use `AuthEnvelopedData` with AES-GCM by default, keeping `EnvelopedData` with AES-CBC for receiving systems that require it.
- Scope: PAdES verification is to count document timestamps as time evidence at B-T and B-LT.
- Scope: JAdES verification is to accept signatures without `x5c` when the signing certificate is available, and to report the missing parameter as a conformance finding.
