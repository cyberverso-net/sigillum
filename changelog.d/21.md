### Added

- The CI check *No crypto-bigint 0.5 or der 0.7 in the library*, `.github/scripts/check-library-graph.sh` with its tests, which fails if `crypto-bigint` 0.5 or `der` 0.7 enters the normal or build dependencies of a library crate, with all features and for every target.
- Tests in `sigillum-pki` and `sigillum-cms` that pin the behaviour of `der` 0.8.3 and `const-oid` 0.10.2 on which the rules for decoding untrusted input depend. ADR-0024 records the findings in a dated note: decoding through `Decode` checks a declared length against the input before allocating, while `Reader::read_vec` still allocates first; `const-oid` 0.10.2 handles arcs from 2^28 to 2^32 - 1 without a panic, but lacks RustCrypto/formats#2412, so the limit of 2^28 on arcs stays.

### Changed

- `sigillum-pki` and `sigillum-cms` depend on the `der` 0.8 generation of the RustCrypto crates set by ADR-0025, with `cms` 0.3.0-rc.0 and `rsa` 0.10.0-rc.19 pinned exactly. `crypto-bigint` resolves to 0.7.10. `sigillum-testpki` stays on the `der` 0.7 generation.
- Dependabot updates `cms` and `rsa` on their own, outside the `rustcrypto-dalek` group, so that each new release candidate arrives in its own pull request. The Dependabot list check enforces this. `cbc` joins the group and its ignore rules.

- `cargo vet` imports the audits of ISRG and Zcash, and trusts the crates.io publishers of the library's dependencies outside the RustCrypto and dalek crates, as described in `docs/development/quality-gates.md`. Ten exemptions of tooling dependencies that the imports and these entries now cover are removed: `autocfg`, `cfg-if`, `ff`, `fiat-crypto`, `flagset`, `group`, `libc`, `libm`, `rand_core` and `typenum`.

### Security

- The acceptance of RUSTSEC-2026-0387 in `deny.toml` now covers `sigillum-testpki` only. The library crates use `crypto-bigint` 0.7, which contains the fix.
