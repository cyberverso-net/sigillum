### Added

- `sigillum-testpki generate crls`, which writes the CRLs of the test PKI under `testdata/crl/` and records each in the manifest, after `generate pki`. Each certificate whose issuer has a current CRL links it in `revocation`. The command keeps the `revocation` links of the CRL entries it replaces, removes the entries of CRLs it no longer produces, with the links to them, and reports files under `testdata/crl/` that the manifest does not name. Generation is reproducible byte for byte.
- The committed CRLs under `testdata/crl/`, built from `x509-cert` CRL structures as ADR-0022 decides: a current CRL for the root CA and for every CA that issues certificates, the misconfigured ones included, and three defective CRLs of the ECDSA CA, one expired, one signed with the wrong key and one with the wrong issuer name.
- Five revoked end-entity certificates in the test PKI: one per signature family revoked for key compromise before the planned signing time, 2029-12-16T10:00:00Z, and one ECDSA certificate revoked after it. `testdata/README.md` lists the timeline of the test PKI.

### Changed

- `sigillum-testpki generate pki` keeps the `revocation` links of the certificate entries it replaces.
