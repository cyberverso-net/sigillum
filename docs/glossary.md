# Glossary

This glossary explains the terms used across the sigillum documentation, for readers who are not specialists in electronic signatures. Each entry gives a short explanation and the source that defines or describes the term. Where the source and this explanation differ, the source prevails.

The legal acts are cited through their ELI links. [Regulation (EU) No 910/2014](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj) of 23 July 2014 (eIDAS) is cited as amended by [Regulation (EU) 2024/1183](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj) of 11 April 2024.

| Term | Explanation | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdES (advanced electronic signature) | An electronic signature that meets four requirements. It is uniquely linked to the signatory and capable of identifying them. It is created with signature creation data that the signatory can, with a high level of confidence, use under their sole control. It is linked to the signed data so that any later change is detectable. CAdES, PAdES, XAdES and JAdES are the ETSI formats for advanced electronic signatures. | Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 ([ELI](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj)), Article 3(11) and Article 26(1) |
| Baseline levels (B-B, B-T, B-LT, B-LTA) | The four levels of an ETSI baseline signature. B-B is the basic signature, with its signed attributes and some unsigned ones. B-T adds a trusted time-stamp proving that the signature existed at a given time. B-LT adds the certificates and revocation data needed to validate it later. B-LTA adds time-stamps that keep this validation material verifiable over the long term: archive time-stamps in CAdES and JAdES, document time-stamps in PAdES. | [ETSI EN 319 122-1 V1.3.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31912201/01.03.01_60/), clause 6.1 (CAdES); [ETSI EN 319 142-1 V1.2.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31914201/01.02.01_60/), clause 6.1 (PAdES); [ETSI TS 119 182-1 V1.2.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/119100_119199/11918201/01.02.01_60/), clause 6.1 (JAdES) |
| BES (basic electronic signature) | The basic form of CAdES and PAdES signature defined by the earlier ETSI specifications, as CAdES-BES and PAdES-BES. sigillum keeps it because some national systems still require it, as [scope.md](project/scope.md) records. The current European Norms define the corresponding levels as CAdES-E-BES and PAdES-E-BES. | [ETSI TS 101 733 V2.2.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/101700_101799/101733/02.02.01_60/), clause 4.3.1 (CAdES-BES); [ETSI TS 102 778-3 V1.2.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/102700_102799/10277803/01.02.01_60/), clause 4 (PAdES-BES); [ETSI EN 319 122-2 V1.1.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31912202/01.01.01_60/), clause 4.3 (CAdES-E-BES); [ETSI EN 319 142-2 V1.2.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31914202/01.02.01_60/), clause 5.3 (PAdES-E-BES) |
| CMS (Cryptographic Message Syntax) | The IETF syntax for signing data (SignedData) and for encrypting it to recipients (EnvelopedData, and AuthEnvelopedData with authenticated encryption). CAdES signatures, and the signatures embedded in PAdES documents, are CMS SignedData structures. | [IETF RFC 5652](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5652.html), sections 5 (SignedData) and 6 (EnvelopedData); [IETF RFC 5083](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5083.html) (AuthEnvelopedData) |
| CRL (certificate revocation list) | A list, signed by a certification authority or a CRL issuer acting for it, of the certificates that have been revoked, with the time of each revocation. | [IETF RFC 5280](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280.html), sections 3.3 and 5 |
| CSC API | The API published by the Cloud Signature Consortium for remote signing. An application sends a signing service the data to be signed, typically the digest of a document, and the service signs it with a key held on the user's behalf. The scope of sigillum includes a signer adapter for it. | Cloud Signature Consortium, [Architectures and protocols for remote signature applications](https://cloudsignatureconsortium.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/csc-api.pdf), version 2.2.0.0 (2025-11-04) |
| Document security store (DSS) | A dictionary in a signed PDF document that holds validation data, such as certificates, CRLs and OCSP responses, for the signatures in the document. PAdES uses it at levels B-LT and B-LTA. Not to be confused with DSS, the Digital Signature Service library of the European Commission. | ETSI EN 319 142-1 V1.2.1, clause 5.4.2 |
| Indication and sub-indication | The result of a signature validation. The indication is TOTAL-PASSED, TOTAL-FAILED or INDETERMINATE; the sub-indication gives the reason for a failed or indeterminate result, for example an expired certificate or missing revocation data. | [ETSI EN 319 102-1 V1.4.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31910201/01.04.01_60/), clause 5.1.3 |
| LOTL (list of trusted lists) | The list published by the European Commission that points to the trusted list of each Member State. It also identifies the certificates used to sign them, and is the starting point for finding the national trusted lists. | [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/119600_119699/119612/02.04.01_60/), clause 5.3.3 and Annex D.5; Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, Article 22(4); Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/1505 of 8 September 2015 ([ELI](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2015/1505/oj)), Article 4(3) and Annex I, Annex I as amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2164 of 27 October 2025 ([ELI](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2025/2164/oj)), which bases the common template on ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 |
| OCSP (Online Certificate Status Protocol) | A protocol for asking a responder whether a certificate is revoked. The signed response gives the status of the certificate (good, revoked or unknown) at a given time. | [IETF RFC 6960](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6960.html), section 2.2 |
| QSCD (qualified electronic signature creation device) | An electronic signature creation device, such as a smart card or a hardware security module, that meets the requirements of Annex II of eIDAS and is certified as such. A qualified electronic signature must be created with one. A remote QSCD is one managed by a qualified trust service provider on behalf of the signatory. | Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, Article 3(12), Article 3(23), Article 3(23a), Article 30 and Annex II |
| Signed and unsigned attributes | Attributes attached to a signature in a CMS SignerInfo. Signed attributes, such as the signing time and the reference to the signing certificate, are covered by the signature value. Unsigned attributes, such as time-stamps and validation data, are added outside it, often after signing, to reach higher levels. | IETF RFC 5652, section 5.3; ETSI EN 319 122-1 V1.3.1, clause 5 |
| Trust anchor | An entity that a relying party trusts directly, represented by a certificate or a public key, from which certification paths are validated. | ETSI EN 319 102-1 V1.4.1, clause 3.1; IETF RFC 5280, section 6.1.1 |
| Trusted list | A list published by a Member State. It names the qualified trust service providers for which the Member State is responsible and the qualified trust services they provide, with their status and status history. Validation uses it to decide whether a certificate or time-stamp comes from a qualified provider. | Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, Article 22(1); Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2015/1505 of 8 September 2015 ([ELI](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2015/1505/oj)), adopted under Article 22(5), Annex I, as amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2164 of 27 October 2025 ([ELI](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2025/2164/oj)), applicable from 29 April 2026. The amendment moves the basis of the common template from ETSI TS 119 612 V2.1.1 to V2.4.1, which is why this entry cites V2.4.1; ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1, clauses 3.1 and 5 |
| TSA (time-stamping authority) | A service that issues time-stamp tokens: signed statements that a given digest existed before a given time. | [IETF RFC 3161](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161.html), section 1; [IETF RFC 5816](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5816.html) |
