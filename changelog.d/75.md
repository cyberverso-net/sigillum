### Added

- The option `--policy aligned|default|<file>` of the validation modes of `tools/dss-oracle`, which selects a built-in policy or a DSS policy file. The aligned policy, the default, is the default policy of DSS 6.5.1 with four documented deltas that ETSI EN 319 102-1 V1.4.1 leaves to the validation policy: Ed25519 is accepted, revocation freshness is checked, unknown critical extensions fail, and `id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck` skips revocation checking. The tool builds it at run time from the policy that DSS ships. ADR-0023 records the decision.
- Divergences DIV-3 to DIV-5 from DSS 6.5.1 in `docs/design/divergences.md`: an OCSP response from a responder without `id-kp-OCSPSigning` accepted, no fallback to a CRL when the covering OCSP response gives the status unknown or its signer cannot be validated, and OUT_OF_BOUNDS_NOT_REVOKED for expired certificates whose revocation data show no status after expiry. Threat K11 of the threat model covers the selection of revocation data.
- Two fixtures that separate the readings of ETSI EN 319 102-1 V1.4.1, clause 5.2.6.4, NOTE 8: `ocsp/signer-expired-before-expiry`, a fresh response for `pki/signer-expired` issued before it expired, and `crl/ca-ecdsa-expired-certs`, a CRL issued after that expiry with the extension expiredCertsOnCRL of ITU-T X.509 (10/2019), clause 9.5.2.8.

### Changed

- `summary.json` of `tools/dss-oracle` has the format version 2, with the member `policy`, which names the validation policy of the run, or the policy file with its SHA-256 digest.
- The corpus test of `validate-certificate` compares every certificate, CRL and OCSP response with its prediction under the aligned policy and fails on any difference that is not a recorded divergence. The results under the default policy of DSS are recorded for information.

### Fixed

- The predictions in `testdata/manifest.toml` for `pki/signer-not-yet-valid` and `ocsp/signer-not-yet-valid` are INDETERMINATE with CERTIFICATE_CHAIN_GENERAL_FAILURE, and for `pki/signer-under-expired-ca`, `crl/ca-expired` and `ocsp/signer-under-expired-ca` INDETERMINATE with REVOCATION_OUT_OF_BOUNDS_NO_POE, as ETSI EN 319 102-1 V1.4.1, clause 5.2.6.4, gives them. The notes of the manifest state the results of DSS 6.5.1 instead of guessing them.
